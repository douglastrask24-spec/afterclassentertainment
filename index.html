Campus rep program

Join the team.
Get a cut.

AfterClass reps are the students who bring their campus out. You get your own promo code, share it with your people, and earn money on every ticket bought with it. No experience needed, just a group chat and a phone.

  1. 1

    DM us

    Message @afterclassaz with your name and school. We'll set up a quick call.

  2. 2

    Get your code

    We make you a personal promo code on Posh. Anyone who uses it at checkout counts toward your sales.

  3. 3

    Promote

    Post your code on your story, in group chats and around campus. We send you flyers and video for every event.

  4. 4

    Get paid

    You earn on every ticket sold with your code, paid out after each event. We go over rates in the DM.

Questions

Before you go.

Q.01When is the first event?

We're locking in the venue and date for Night One now. Join the waitlist on Posh to hear first, and follow @afterclassaz on Instagram.

Q.02Do I need to be a student?

Our nights are built for ASU and GCU students, but anyone 18 or older with a valid physical ID can come.

Q.03Where will tickets be sold?

Every ticket is sold on our Posh page. Prices go up in tiers as each event fills, so buy early.

Q.04Will there be VIP tickets?

Yes. Each event's Posh page will list what VIP includes when tickets go live.

Q.05I DJ. How do I get booked?

DM @afterclassaz your mix link and a short bio. We book local and student DJs for every event.
